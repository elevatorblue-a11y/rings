How to play

Three moves, all thumbs

If you've ever held the plastic toy with the little water tank, you already know how. If you haven't, it takes about ten seconds to learn.

  1. Send

    Press the two round buttons. Every press sends a jet of bubbles up through the water and lifts the rings.

  2. Tilt

    Tip your phone to steer. The water stays level, so the rings slide the way you lean.

  3. Land

    Float a ring over a nose, fin, horn or tail and let it settle. Land all five rings to finish the level.

Seven worlds

Seventy levels, and no two alike

Every world has ten creatures and a bonus round. Each level adds its own twist: ocean currents, quicksand, desert wind, low gravity, rainstorms, fog, floating ice and more.

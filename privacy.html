Privacy Policy

The short version: Rings! doesn't collect anything about the people who play it.

Rings! is a game made by Vincent McAninch for kids and families. This page explains what happens to information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. Rings! has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't use any outside services that collect data.

What stays on your device

Your progress, best scores, Hall of Fame initials and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple only tells the game whether it has been unlocked. Apple's own privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Rings! is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. A grown-up check appears before any purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email elevatorblue@icloud.com.

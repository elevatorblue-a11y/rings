Send bubbles up. Land the rings.

Rings! is the classic water ring-toss toy, made for your phone. Press the bubble buttons, tilt to steer, and float all five rings onto friendly creatures in seven worlds.

  • 70 levels
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • Made for families

What this tests

The hero's three phones, each playing a 9-second gameplay loop instead of a still picture, cycling as on the home page: the Ocean (553 KB), the Rain Forest (876 KB) and the Dinosaurs (632 KB). The iPhone frames themselves are unchanged. With "Reduce Motion" turned on, the still pictures show instead.

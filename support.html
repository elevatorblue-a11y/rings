Rings! Help

How can we help?

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, email us and we'll write back.

How do I play?

Press the two round buttons to pump bubbles and push the water. Tilt your phone to steer the rings. Land all five rings on the creatures to finish the level.

What's free?

The first five Ocean levels. After that, one $3.99 purchase unlocks the whole game: all 7 worlds, plus every new world we add, at no extra cost.

How do I get my purchases on a new phone?

Tap the gear button, then Levels & Store, then Restore Purchases at the bottom. Purchases come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

My child asked to buy a world. What happens?

Before any purchase, a grown-up answers a times-table question written out in words. If Ask to Buy is on for your family, the request then goes to the parent or guardian, and the game unlocks by itself as soon as they approve it.

How do I turn the music, sounds or vibration down?

Tap the gear button. Music, sound effects and vibration each have their own setting.

How do I start over?

Tap the gear button, then Start over at Level 1. Your purchases stay unlocked.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Something isn't working?

Email mcaninch.vincent@gmail.com with your iPhone model and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
